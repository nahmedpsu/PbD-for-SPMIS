# Code of Conduct

This project serves people who depend on social protection, often in vulnerable circumstances.
We expect everyone participating here to act with the same respect for others that the software
is designed to give to beneficiaries.

- Be welcoming and patient; assume good faith; explain rather than dismiss.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks in any project space.
- Never share real personal data in issues, pull requests, fixtures or discussions.
- Disagree about design in the open and with evidence; decisions are recorded as ADRs.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting tools or by
contacting the repository owner. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove,
edit or reject contributions and participants that do not follow this code.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
